- (direction by user) Build the largest practical time-series sample for every data source, targeting official coverage back to calendar year 2000 wherever the source permits.
- (direction by user) Expand ACS to retain every official annual 1-year value available and support both year-over-year and five-year change analysis.
- (decision) Treat a five-year change as the difference between two comparable estimates whose end years are five years apart, not as five adjacent annual changes.
- (decision) Treat year-over-year change as the difference between adjacent annual 1-year estimates; retain the two source estimates, product, period, geography, table, variable, and uncertainty rather than storing an opaque result alone.
- (evidence) Census official ACS 1-year API catalog covers 2005-2024, subject to no standard 2020 1-year release; the official 5-year catalog covers 2005-2009 through 2020-2024, with annual release end-years 2009-2024.
- (constraint) ACS 1-year and 5-year estimates are separate products and must never be silently combined or substituted; one-year geography coverage is restricted to Census-published eligible areas while five-year supports small-area geography.
- (constraint) Only non-overlapping five-year ACS periods may be used for a standard five-year change, such as 2015-2019 versus 2020-2024; overlapping consecutive five-year releases are retained but not presented as valid independent changes.
- (constraint) Every acquisition must pass the existing capacity gate, use original Census bytes in DATA_ROOT, preserve artifact checksum and ingest-run evidence, and be resumable and idempotent.
- (constraint) Variable, table, universe, geography, and methodology changes must be recorded and must block an automated comparison until a documented compatible mapping or an explicit analyst-facing limitation exists.
- (non-goal) This specification does not authorize a 2000 ACS substitute, experimental 2020 ACS 1-year estimates, a bulk download, schema migration, or a claim that every source reaches 2000 before a source-specific availability audit.
- (event) Self-validate pass 1: four capabilities each have testable intent and success; constraints preserve product separation, availability boundaries, comparability, and provenance; non-goals prevent unapproved acquisition and false continuity.
- (event) Self-validate pass 2: preserved the operator's year-2000 target, maximum official annual coverage request, 1-year expansion, year-over-year and five-year semantics, 2020 gap, and active-job non-interruption; no wrapper-only claim was retained.
