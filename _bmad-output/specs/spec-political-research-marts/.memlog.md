---
topic: Provenance-backed political research marts
goal: Provide stable researcher-facing political analysis views without exposing raw ingestion tables or hiding coverage limits
updated: 2026-10-02T14:31
---

- (direction by user) Make the database easy for political researchers to use and able to connect money, politicians, office history, legislation, votes, and place-based conditions without forcing users to rebuild joins.
- (decision) Research marts are derived, documented views built over owned core/fact rows; they do not replace canonical facts, source evidence, or the identity gate.
- (capability) Researchers can use named politician, office-term, member-vote, bill, candidate-cycle finance, and district-year marts with an explicit row grain, time basis, coverage, and evidence drill-through.
- (capability) Each mart exposes source/coverage flags so an unavailable source, unresolved person, incomplete jurisdiction, or non-comparable geography cannot be mistaken for a measured zero.
- (constraint) Marts use dbt and must preserve one declared grain; they cannot create cross-source person links by name, attach a historical fact to a current district boundary, or hide provenance in an opaque score.
- (non-goal) This work does not create a single political effectiveness, bias, integrity, or corruption score; researchers may calculate transparent measures from documented fields later.
- (assumption) Marts are published only as their dependent source coverage becomes complete; a catalog can document a planned mart before its data is available.
- (event) Coherence validation passed: four capabilities have testable success, constraints preserve dbt ownership and identity/geography safeguards, and non-goals prohibit false coverage and opaque scores.
- (event) Preservation validation passed: usable political research access, coverage visibility, stable grains, evidence drill-through, historical geography, and non-opaque analytical measures are represented in SPEC.md or mart-catalog.md.
